Your voice agent is fast. Is it listening when the caller changes their mind?

A small lab for one failure mode that latency dashboards hide: the caller corrects themselves, and the agent has already acted on the first sentence. Tune the pipeline, play the call, and see whether the transfer goes to the right account.

The caller says Move two thousand dollars to savings, pauses, then adds wait, no, to checking. The agent calls initiate_transfer() as soon as it thinks the turn is over.

What the lab is modeling

The end-of-turn silence wait is the real trade-off. Shorten it and the agent answers sooner, but it also commits to a sentence the caller had not finished. Lengthen it and callers feel the dead air.

Barge-in detection only helps with what the agent is saying. It does nothing about what the agent has already done. For read-only answers that is fine. For money movement, the tool call has to be staged and cancellable, or the fastest pipeline is also the one that sends funds to the wrong account.
